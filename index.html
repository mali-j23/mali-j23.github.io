Professional practice, Connecticut Department of Public Health, summer 2026

Connecticut's ischemic stroke death rate rose more than twice as fast as the nation's, 2018–2024

The question
Is Connecticut's stroke mortality following the national trend, and which stroke subtype explains any difference?
What I found
Ischemic stroke drove the state's rise. Hemorrhagic stroke fell at about the same pace as it did nationally, so it wasn't the cause.
+15.4%per year, Connecticut
+6.7%per year, United States
Why it matters
Connecticut's rates are still below the national level, but they are moving faster in the wrong direction. That points prevention and surveillance toward ischemic stroke risk factors, and toward finding which towns and counties drive the trend.

Professional practice, Connecticut Department of Public Health, spring 2026

In the 2024 BRFSS, the lowest-income adults in Connecticut had about twice the odds of reported heart disease

The question
How common is reported heart disease among Connecticut adults, and how does it vary by income, behavior, and access to care?
What I found
Weighted prevalence was 8.75%. Odds rose as income fell (OR 2.19 under $25K vs. $75K or more), and were higher for current smokers (1.50) and adults with obesity (1.38).
A result to read carefully
Adults with a personal doctor had 3.2 times the odds of reporting heart disease. That most likely reflects who gets diagnosed, so the survey probably undercounts disease among people without regular care.
Read the BRFSS heart disease study

Academic research, undergraduate thesis, Bahria University, 2024

Practitioners in Islamabad and Rawalpindi were ready for telemedicine. Most didn't know enough about it yet.

The question
What do healthcare practitioners know about telemedicine, how do they see it, and would they adopt it?
What I found
In a survey of 120 practitioners, three in four were positive about telemedicine and willing to use it, but three in four also scored as having poor knowledge of it.
Why it matters
Where attitude is already favorable, training is the more likely lever for adoption.
