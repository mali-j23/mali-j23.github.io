Connecticut Department of Public Health, summer 2026

Stroke death rates in Connecticut and the U.S.

Connecticut's overall stroke mortality looked like a slow climb. Splitting it by subtype showed ischemic stroke deaths rising 15.4% a year, against 6.7% nationally, while hemorrhagic stroke fell.

How: CDC WONDER death data, ICD-10 subtype codes, age-adjusted rates, Joinpoint regression. Presented with three recommendations to CTDPH staff and leadership.

Read the study

Connecticut Department of Public Health, spring 2026

Heart disease and income in the 2024 BRFSS

Among 7,213 Connecticut adults, those earning under $25K had about twice the odds of reported heart disease as those earning $75K or more. The odds rose step by step as income fell.

How: survey-weighted analysis in SAS (PROC SURVEYFREQ, PROC SURVEYLOGISTIC) using the BRFSS strata, clusters and final weights. Presented to CTDPH staff. SAS code on the study page.

Read the study

Undergraduate thesis, Bahria University, 2024

Telemedicine readiness among practitioners

Three in four practitioners were willing to use telemedicine, and three in four scored low on knowledge of it. The barrier was knowledge, not willingness.

How: cross-sectional survey of 120 practitioners in Islamabad and Rawalpindi, built on the Technology Acceptance Model and analyzed in SPSS.
